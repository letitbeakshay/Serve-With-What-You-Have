How it works

You bring what you have. We do the connecting.

Three steps, and the hardest one is already behind you if you have decided to give something.

STEP 1

Tell us what you can give

Clothes, an hour, a skill, a seat in your car. Whatever you have, and roughly where you are.

STEP 2

We look for who needs it

We speak to homes, schools and community groups, and find the ones asking for what you have.

STEP 3

We put you in touch

You take it from there together, and agree between you what happens and when.

It is not we who serve. You are the one serving the people who need it. We only stand in the middle and bring the two of you together.

Why we exist

Ask people why they do not serve, and most of them say the same word.

Money. They are waiting until there is enough of it, and that day keeps moving.

So the clothes stay in the cupboard. The free Saturday passes. The skill sits unused. Not because anyone is unwilling, but because they are measuring what they have against the one thing they think it takes.

It was never the only way. A home near you needs shoes in size five, someone to sit with the children on a Sunday, an hour of spoken English on a weeknight. None of that is bought.

That is the gap we stand in. We look for the people who are asking for something, and connect them with the people who already have it.

Questions

The things people ask first.

How does sharing clothes work?

You tell us what you have and roughly where you are. We find a home or group that has asked for it, and put you in touch so the two of you can arrange the rest.

Do I need to pay anything?

No. Using this site is free. We do not collect money, process payments, or accept donations of any kind.

Are you a registered NGO or charity?

No. We are a community initiative, not a registered organisation. We cannot issue tax receipts, and we do not solicit or receive funds.

What do you do with my details?

We store your name, city and how you would like to serve, so we can tell you about opportunities near you. We do not sell your details or share them without asking. You can ask us to delete everything at any time.

Can I give money to someone through this site?

Not at the moment. There is no payment option on this site yet. If we later find that what someone needs really is money, and we are able to help with that properly, we will look at adding it. For now it is not available.

One last thing

What do you have today?

Not next month. Not once things settle down. Today, with whatever is already in the house.
